SAMPLE DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT - NOT AN OFFICIAL UNIVERSITY PROCEDURE
Title: Account Security Guidance (Sample)

Problem: I think someone else has accessed my account

1. Change your password immediately from a device you trust.
2. Sign out of all other sessions if the system offers that option.
3. Check your recent sign-in activity for places or devices you do not recognize.
4. Create a support ticket right away and describe what you noticed.

Problem: I received a suspicious email asking for my password

1. Do not click links or open attachments in the email.
2. Never share your password, even with someone who claims to be from IT.
3. Delete the email or report it as phishing, and tell the IT helpdesk by creating a support ticket.

Problem: How to keep my account safe

1. Use a long, unique password that you do not use anywhere else.
2. Turn on two-step verification if your university account supports it.
3. Do not save your password on shared or lab computers.
4. Sign out when you finish using a shared computer.
