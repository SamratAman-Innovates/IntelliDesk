SAMPLE DEMONSTRATION DOCUMENT - NOT AN OFFICIAL UNIVERSITY PROCEDURE
Title: Computer Lab Support (Sample)

Problem: The lab computer will not start or is frozen

1. Check that the monitor and the computer are both switched on and the cables are firmly connected.
2. If the screen is frozen, wait one minute, then hold the power button to restart the computer.
3. Do not unplug hardware or open the computer case.
4. Report the computer number and the problem to the lab assistant, or create a support ticket if no assistant is available.

Problem: I cannot sign in to a lab computer or the lab software is missing

1. Sign in with your own university account and check that Caps Lock is off.
2. Try another computer in the lab to see whether the problem affects only one machine.
3. Do not install software on lab computers yourself.
4. Report the computer number and the missing software to the lab assistant or create a support ticket.

Problem: How to book a lab slot or ask for technical help

1. Lab booking rules differ between departments, so follow the booking instructions announced by your department.
2. For technical problems during a booked slot, tell the lab assistant first and create a support ticket if the problem is not fixed.
