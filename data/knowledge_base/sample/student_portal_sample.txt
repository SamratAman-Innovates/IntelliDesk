Title: Student Portal Login Help (Sample)

Problem: The student portal says my username or password is incorrect

1. Check that Caps Lock is off and that you typed your student ID exactly as issued.
2. Use the Forgot Password link on the portal login page to reset your password.
3. After a reset, wait a few minutes before signing in again.
4. Clear your browser cache or try a private browsing window.
5. If the portal still rejects your login, create a support ticket with your student ID and the error message.

Problem: The student portal page does not load or shows an error

1. Check that your internet connection works by opening another website.
2. Try a different browser or update your current browser.
3. Disable browser extensions that block scripts, then reload the page.
4. If the page still fails, note the error message and create a support ticket.
