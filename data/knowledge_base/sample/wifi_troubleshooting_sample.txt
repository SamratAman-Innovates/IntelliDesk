Title: Wi-Fi Troubleshooting Guide (Sample)

Problem: Wi-Fi is connected but there is no internet access

1. Turn Wi-Fi off on your device, wait ten seconds, and turn it on again.
2. Forget the campus network in your Wi-Fi settings, then reconnect and enter your credentials again.
3. Check that the date and time on your device are correct. Wrong time settings can block secure connections.
4. Restart your device and try again.
5. If other devices also have no internet, the problem may be with the network itself. Report it to the IT helpdesk.

Problem: The campus network does not appear in the list of available networks

1. Make sure Wi-Fi is turned on and Airplane mode is off.
2. Move closer to an access point, since walls and distance weaken the signal.
3. Restart your device to refresh the network list.
4. If the network still does not appear, create a support ticket with your location and device type.
